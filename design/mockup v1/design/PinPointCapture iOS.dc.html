Turn 1

PinPointCapture — iOS & iPadOS

A first pass at the app, weighted towards the two areas you called out: first-run setup and the link to a Studio host. Dark, system-native iOS throughout — a camera tool that lives on a tripod in a bay or at a range, where a light UI would glare and a bright screen behind the golfer is a distraction. One tint (system green) carries state; red, orange and blue are used only in their system meanings.

What I assumed

Onboarding never assumes a host is nearby (UC-1 is the normal case). The offline path is offered as a first-class choice, not a fallback. Permissions are asked one screen at a time with the reason stated in plain terms, since a single Local Network refusal makes the app look broken.

The system

SF for everything, SF Mono for any number a golfer could not have guessed — offsets, residuals, frame intervals. Grouped inset lists, large titles, 50pt filled buttons, 44pt minimum targets. Green means retained and in sync; orange means degraded but capturing; red means the host is gone; capture status never turns red.

Open, for you

Audio retention (OPEN-2) is shown as a visible, changeable setting during onboarding — worth confirming that wording. Markup tools, the diagnostic bundle, review mode and voice club tagging are named but not designed yet. Placement diagrams are placeholders; real illustration or photography would carry those screens better.

Where I would go next

The obvious gaps, in the order I would close them: the markup tools as a real interaction rather than three buttons; two-shot comparison, which is the most-used review feature and the least designed here; review mode for App Review; the diagnostic bundle; and the sensor pairing flow once IMU lands. Placement and framing would both benefit from real illustration.

Easy things to ask for: a light-mode variant of the non-camera screens · onboarding condensed to four screens · a warmer, less clinical treatment of the telemetry · the whole onboarding flow as a tappable prototype.
